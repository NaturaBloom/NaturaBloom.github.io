Frequently Asked Questions｜常见问题

1. Is this product soy free? / 是否不含大豆？

EN: Yes. This vitamin E formula is 100% soy free, sourced from sunflower oil, palm oil and rice oil.

中文：完全无大豆配方，原料取自向日葵、棕榈、米糠植物油，规避大豆过敏源。

2. When is the best time to take it? / 最佳服用时间？

EN: One capsule per day after meals is recommended for better absorption.

中文：建议每日餐后服用一粒，油脂环境更利于维生素E吸收。

3. Is it suitable for sensitive people? / 敏感人群可用吗？

EN: No artificial pigments, no preservatives, hypoallergenic plant capsules.

中文：无色素、无防腐剂、植物胶囊低敏配方，敏感体质友好。

4. Can pregnant women take it? / 孕妇能否食用？

EN: Pregnant or breastfeeding women need to consult a doctor before use.

中文：孕期、哺乳期女性请遵从医师指导后食用。
